About

I am a computational scientist with a PhD in Applied Science and over nine years of experience applying machine learning, causal inference, and multi-omic data integration to genomics, neurodegenerative disease, and cardiovascular research.

My work translates complex computational findings for clinical and academic collaborators, builds reproducible analytical pipelines, and mentors junior researchers across academic and clinical research settings. I hold a PhD in Applied Science from the American University in Cairo, where my dissertation focused on multi-omic (proteomic and metabolomic) biomarker discovery in Parkinson's Disease, and an MSc in Bioinformatics & Computational Biology, also from AUC. I earned my BSc in Pharmacy and Biotechnology from the German University in Cairo.
